homemade crayons

domače voščenke · product and kids' workshop · Green Lab, Rog Creative Center

A sustainable set of crayons made from used cooking oil and beeswax, coloured with natural pigments such as turmeric, sweet paprika, beetroot, cocoa and soil.

We designed the moulds by 3D-printing a positive and casting a silicone negative from it. The packaging is just as simple: sawdust bound together with corn starch and pressed into a mould that we also 3D-printed.

The crayons are also a workshop that I lead, where kids make their own set of crayons.
