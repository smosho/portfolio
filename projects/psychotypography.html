ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUWXYZ

the anti-book · Psychotypography workshop, ALUO UL, 2024

ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUWXYZ is the final result of the “Psychotypography” workshop, led by Žiga Testen at ALUO UL in December 2024. It contains participants’ typographic manifestos, overlaid with their notes and photos taken by Ben Morris.

The publication layers two body texts: one boldens into illegible blackness, the other one thins and disappears into the page, while photos interrupt their rhythm. It becomes an absurd, unusable object that explores what a book is through escalating illegibility and unreadability.

Selected for the 12th Brumen Biennial of Slovenian Design and received the Brumen award.

team: Eva Bevec, Nik Leban, Lenart Marovt, Vanja Flis
