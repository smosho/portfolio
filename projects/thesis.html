bacterial 3D printing

master's thesis in biochemistry · FKKT UL, 2026

For my master's thesis in biochemistry I developed an alginate-based material for 3D printing that hardens after extrusion through microbially induced calcium carbonate precipitation (MICP). Lyophilised Sporosarcina pasteurii bacteria in the material turn it into stone-like calcium carbonate when it is incubated in a mineralisation solution.

The work combined materials engineering, microbiology and biochemistry, from the rheology of the printing paste to printing and mineralising increasingly complex shapes.
