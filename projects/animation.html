visualising biomineralisation

3D animation in Blender · Advanced Computer Graphics, NTF UL 2025/26

A short animation made in Blender that visualises the process of biomineralisation from my master's thesis, in four scenes: the biochemical process at the scale of bacteria, mixing the material and adding calcium before pouring it into a mould, calcium reacting with carbonate ions, and finally taking the hardened piece out of the mould.

The project covers the basics of 3D animation: modelling, materials, lighting, camera work and fluid simulation.
