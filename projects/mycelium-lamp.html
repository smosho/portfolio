mycelium lamp

lučka iz micelija · workshop for adults · Green Lab, Rog Creative Center

A lampshade grown from mushroom mycelium. The mycelium is first grown on agar, then on grain and finally on a sawdust substrate, which is packed into a mould – in this case a spherical bowl – and left to grow into a dome.

A 3D-printed piece that holds the light bulb is grown into the top of the shade, so the mycelium itself becomes the lampshade.

It is also a workshop that I lead for adults, where participants grow their own mycelium lamp.
