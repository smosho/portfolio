Grasshopper · Galapagos · 2025/26

A series of exercises in evolutionary modelling, made in Grasshopper with the Galapagos solver for the course Digital Modelling and Fabrication. Instead of designing a form directly, I set up a parametric model and a goal, and let the solver evolve the form: first simple form studies, then maximising and minimising direct sunlight on generated volumes, and optimising street width and tower heights for the best view in a city block.

For the final project I generated surfaces for extrusion 3D-printing the bacterial material from my master's thesis. The material needs to hold onto the growth medium on its surface for the bacteria to stay active and mineralise it, so the surfaces get a slope-dependent texture. The final model was 3D-printed in PLA for demonstration.
