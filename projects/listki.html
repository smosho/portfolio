listki

porcelain leaves

Listki (little leaves) are jewellery made from real leaves cast in porcelain. Leaves picked up in the forest, along the pavement or in the garden are dipped in liquid porcelain until the coating is strong enough, then left to dry in the air. Many of them crack, curl or fall apart at this stage.

In the kiln the organic leaf burns away and leaves behind a porcelain fossil of itself. The surviving leaves are finished with silver chains and rings into necklaces, earrings and other pieces.

I also designed and built the Listki website, which shows the collection and the process.
